Indicates when UART and the RF radio are ready to accept transmit data. 


\begin{DoxyParams}[1]{Parameters}

\hline
\end{DoxyParams}
\begin{DoxyReturn}{Returns}
true if transmission may proceed, false otherwise.
\end{DoxyReturn}
Typical considerations include:\+
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item UART buffer availability (e.\+g., Serial.\+available\+For\+Write()).
\item RF radio-\/ready signals (e.\+g., AUX pin on E22).
\item The configured Radio\+Pkt\+Max\+Bytes value.
\item The radio\textquotesingle{}s specific header requirments (e.\+g. address, channel, etc...) ~\newline

\end{DoxyItemize}

\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
It is the implementers responsibility to send the radio its appropriate header just before returning true. The protocol will always provide the destination address of the packet by passing it to {\ttfamily \+\_\+\+To\+RFAddr} on every call. Address 0x\+FFFF will be passed if there is no destination address. This allows radio usage flexibility. 

If Radio\+Pkt\+Max\+Bytes is known to always be less than the UART buffer depth, the implementer may choose to wait until enough space is available in the UART buffer to signal ready. Otherwise, simply checking that the UART buffer is not full is sufficient. 
\end{DoxyNote}
